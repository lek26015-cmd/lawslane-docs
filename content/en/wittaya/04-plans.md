Every account starts on the **Free** plan. Each plan includes different features:

| Feature | Free | Premium / Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Take exams | Limited sets per day | Unlimited |
| AI grading of written answers | As set by the team | Yes |
| View model answers | Yes | Yes |

> The team can adjust the limits for each plan. See the latest numbers on the **Plans** (แพ็กเกจ) page on the site.

## Upgrade your plan

You cannot subscribe to Premium/Pro yourself on the site yet. To upgrade, [contact the team](/help/contact), and the team will enable it on your account directly. Some plans have an expiry date. When a plan expires, your account goes back to Free.
