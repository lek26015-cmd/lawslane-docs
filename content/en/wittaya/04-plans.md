Every account starts on the **Free** plan. Each plan includes different features (defaults):

| Feature | Free | Premium | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Take exams | 3 sets per day | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| AI grading of written answers | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI weakness analysis | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| View model answers | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| No ads | – | Yes | Yes |
| Free download of exam-collection E-Books | – | – | Yes |

> The team can adjust the features of each plan. To see what your account actually has, check the plan status box on the **My learning** (การเรียนรู้ของฉัน) page (see [My learning and account](/wittaya/my-learning)) and the line under the **Start exam now** (เริ่มทำข้อสอบทันที) button on the exam details page.

Premium and Pro users have a colored ring and a plan badge around their profile picture (Premium in blue-violet, Pro in gold). Free users do not.

## Upgrade your plan

You cannot subscribe to Premium/Pro yourself on the site yet. To upgrade, [contact the team](/help/contact), and the team will enable it on your account directly. Some plans have an expiry date. When a plan expires, your account goes back to Free.
