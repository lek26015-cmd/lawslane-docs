title: Order books
description: Choose books, add them to the cart, transfer payment, attach the slip, and track your parcel
---

## Choose books

Go to the **Books** (หนังสือ) menu. You can search by title, filter by **Type** (ประเภท) and **Level** (ระดับชั้น), or sort by price or newest. Click **View details** (ดูรายละเอียด) to see information about a book.

- **Add to cart** (ใส่ตะกร้า) — Keep it in the cart and keep browsing
- **Buy now** (ซื้อเลย) — Add it to the cart and open the cart right away

Click the floating cart button on the screen to change quantities or remove items, then click **Checkout →** (ชำระเงิน →).

> The cart is saved in the browser on the device you are using. If you switch devices or browsers, the cart does not follow you.

## Payment

1. Fill in the **Shipping details** (ข้อมูลการจัดส่ง): full name, phone number, and address
2. Transfer the total amount to the account shown on the payment page. **Always check the account name before transferring**
3. Attach an image of the slip in the **Proof of transfer** (หลักฐานการโอนเงิน) field (image file, up to 5MB)
4. Click **Confirm order** (ยืนยันการสั่งซื้อ) (login required)

The team will check the slip and confirm your order.

## Track your order

Go to **Manage account** (จัดการบัญชี) → **Order history** (ประวัติการสั่งซื้อ) → **View all** (ดูทั้งหมด). Orders move through these statuses: Awaiting payment (รอชำระเงิน) → Paid (ชำระแล้ว) → Shipping (กำลังจัดส่ง) → Completed (เสร็จสิ้น).

Click **View details & track parcel** (ดูรายละเอียด & ติดตามพัสดุ) to see the tracking number (click to copy), the carrier, and the status timeline. Then check the tracking number on that carrier's website.
