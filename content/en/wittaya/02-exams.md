## Find exams

Go to the **Exam library** (คลังข้อสอบ), which you can browse without logging in.

- **Year-level tabs** — All, Years 1–3, Year 4 & Lawyer's license
- **Search box** — Type a subject or exam name. Click **Clear** (ล้าง) to start over
- **Subject categories** — Click a subject, or click **View all subjects** (ดูทุกวิชา) to show everything

Each exam card shows the number of questions and the academic year, with two buttons: **Take exam →** (ทำข้อสอบ →) and **View answers** (ดูเฉลย). The card also has badges showing:

- **Exam type** — Written (อัตนัย), Multiple choice (ปรนัย), or Multiple choice + Written (ปรนัย + อัตนัย)
- The exam **level**
- If you are logged in, **Not taken yet** (ยังไม่เคยทำ) or **Taken N times · Best score X** (ทำแล้ว N ครั้ง · คะแนนสูงสุด X)

## Take an exam

You must log in before taking an exam.

1. Click **Take exam →** (ทำข้อสอบ →). The timer starts immediately
2. For **multiple-choice questions**, click an option. For **written questions**, type your answer in the box
3. Use **← Previous** (← ข้อก่อนหน้า) / **Next →** (ข้อถัดไป →), or click a question number in the grid to jump to it. Answered questions turn green
4. The remaining time is shown at the top. When less than 5 minutes remain, the number turns red
5. Click **Submit exam** (ส่งข้อสอบ) when you finish. If time runs out, the system submits automatically

If submission fails, you will see **Submission failed** (ส่งข้อสอบไม่สำเร็จ) with the reason. Your answers stay on the page, so you can click **Submit exam** (ส่งข้อสอบ) again.

> **Caution:** Do not refresh or close the page during the exam, because all unsubmitted answers will be lost.

To read the rules or exam time before starting, open the exam details page, click **Start exam now** (เริ่มทำข้อสอบทันที), tick to accept the rules, then click **Start exam** (เริ่มทำข้อสอบ). If you have taken that set before, the details page has a **View latest result** (ดูผลครั้งล่าสุด) link that opens your most recent result.

On the exam page, you cannot copy, print, or save the page, to protect the exam copyright.

## Daily quota

The number of exam sets you can take per day depends on your plan (see [Plans and features](/wittaya/plans)).

- Free users see a bar at the top of the exam library and the exam page saying how many sets are left today (เหลือสิทธิ์ทำข้อสอบอีก N ชุดวันนี้) or that today's quota is used up (ใช้สิทธิ์ทำข้อสอบครบแล้ววันนี้)
- On the exam details page, the line under **Start exam now** (เริ่มทำข้อสอบทันที) shows your plan and remaining sets, for example "Free plan: 3 sets per day · 2 left today" (แพ็กเกจ Free: ทำข้อสอบได้วันละ 3 ชุด · เหลืออีก 2 ชุดวันนี้)
- Opening the same exam set again on the same day counts as one
- The quota resets at midnight, Thailand time
- When you have used your quota, the exam page shows an upgrade screen with **View Premium plans** (ดูแพ็กเกจ Premium) and **Back to exam library** (กลับไปคลังข้อสอบ) buttons
