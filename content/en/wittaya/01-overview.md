Lawslane Wittaya ([wittaya.lawslane.com](https://wittaya.lawslane.com)) is a law exam prep site in the Lawslane family. The main menu at the top includes:

| Menu | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Exam library** (คลังข้อสอบ) | Take past exams with a timer, have AI grade written answers, and view model answers |
| **Books** (หนังสือ) | Buy exam prep books |
| **Articles** (บทความ) | Read legal articles |
| **Plans** (แพ็กเกจ) | See what each plan includes |
| **My learning** (การเรียนรู้ของฉัน) | See your exam history and purchased books (shown after login) |

> The **Courses** (คอร์สเรียน) section is not open yet. If you click it, you will see a "Coming soon" (เร็วๆ นี้) page.

## Getting started

1. Click **Sign up** (สมัครสมาชิก) at the top right. Choose **Sign up with Google** (สมัครสมาชิกด้วย Google) or enter your name, email, and password (at least 8 characters)
2. Choose **Exam library** (คลังข้อสอบ), then pick the exam you want to take
3. When you finish, the system shows your score and feedback right away

If you forget your password, enter your email on the login page first, then click **Forgot password?** (ลืมรหัสผ่าน?). The system will email you a link to set a new password.
