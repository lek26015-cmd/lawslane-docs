---
title: Client dashboard
description: Track cases, appointments, interpreter jobs, CapDeal contracts, and reported issues in one place
---

Go to **Dashboard** from the profile picture menu. This page includes:

- **Ongoing Cases** — cases still open; tap to go straight to the case chat room
- **Closed Cases** — cases that have been closed
- **Upcoming Appointments** (นัดหมายที่กำลังจะมาถึง) — consultations you've requested with lawyers, nearest first
- **My interpreter bookings** (งานล่ามของฉัน) — your interpreter requests and jobs, plus **Chats with interpreters** (แชทกับล่าม, shown once you've chatted with an interpreter)
- **CapDeal — recent contracts** (แคปดีล — สัญญาล่าสุด) — contracts created on capdeal.lawslane.com
- **Reported Issue Tickets** — issues you've reported to the team, and their latest status
