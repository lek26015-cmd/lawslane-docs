title: Consult a lawyer by chat
description: Message a lawyer, discuss details, share documents, and receive a quotation and engagement contract
---

## Start a consultation

1. On the lawyer's profile page, tap **Send Message** (if you're not logged in, you'll be asked to log in first)
2. Describe your situation in the **Message to lawyer** (ข้อความถึงทนาย) box, then tap **Send message to lawyer** (ส่งข้อความถึงทนาย)
3. A chat room with that lawyer opens

> An initial consultation by chat is **free of charge**. If you need the lawyer to do further work, they will quote a price in the chat

## In the chat room

- **Details** (รายละเอียด) — case information and a summary of fees
- **Document Vault** — upload and view files shared with the lawyer, such as contracts, evidence, or photos
- **Lawyer fees** (ค่าบริการทนายความ) — a card in the sidebar listing the fees, with a **Pay ฿…** (ชำระ ฿…) button on each unpaid item. Lawyer fees are paid into Lawslane's account only, and Lawslane pays the lawyer after the case is closed

## Quotation and engagement contract

Once you reach an agreement, the lawyer sends 2 documents into the chat.

- **📄 New quotation** (เอกสารใบเสนอราคาใหม่) — states the subject and the total, which may be a single payment or split into installments. Tap **📄 View quotation details** (เปิดดูรายละเอียดใบเสนอราคา) to see the details
- **📄 Draft lawyer engagement contract** (ร่างสัญญาจ้างทนายความ) — tap **📄 View contract details** (เปิดดูรายละเอียดสัญญา) to read the scope of work, fees, and terms

A **💳 Pay for the service** (ชำระค่าบริการ) card also arrives. Tap **💳 Pay through Lawslane** (ชำระค่าบริการผ่าน Lawslane) to go to the payment page

1. Read the quotation and contract in full
2. If you agree, tap **Click to sign** (คลิกเพื่อเซ็นชื่อ) and draw your signature
3. Pay the fee into Lawslane's account following the steps in [Pay a lawyer's fees](/client/payment) (never transfer to the lawyer's personal account)

The contract takes full effect once the first installment has been paid in full (if you pay it in parts, once all parts are paid).

## Closing the case and rating

When the work is done, the lawyer sends a summary and closes the case. You'll get an email that the case is closed, with a link to rate it, and the chat room shows a **Rate the service** (ให้คะแนนบริการ) card where you pick a rating and tap **Submit review** (ส่งรีวิว). Your review helps other clients choose a lawyer more easily.

> If the lawyer's final fee is higher than originally agreed, you'll first get a request to pay the difference. See [Additional fees](/client/payment)
