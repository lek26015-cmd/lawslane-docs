## LAlin (ลลิน), the AI legal assistant

LAlin is a floating button called **AI Legal Assistant** in the corner of the screen on most pages of lawslane.com. You can use it without logging in.

1. Tap the **AI Legal Assistant** button
2. Describe what happened, or upload an image such as a chat screenshot or a document. You can also pick one of the suggested sample questions
3. Read the answer, and keep asking follow-up questions

> LAlin is an AI that gives general legal information. It is **not advice from a lawyer**. If you need to make a real decision, [consult a lawyer](/client/find-lawyer). Read more in the [AI disclaimer](https://lawslane.com/th/ai-disclaimer)

## Law Search

Go to **Law Search** (the link is in the site footer). Describe your situation in plain language, then tap **Search** (ค้นหา). You'll see the relevant legal provisions, each tagged **Highly relevant / Relevant / Related** (ตรงมาก / ตรง / เกี่ยวข้อง).

## Legal Forms

The **Legal Forms** menu is a library of downloadable forms. Without logging in you can download 3 forms. Once logged in, downloads are unlimited.
