## Find a lawyer

1. Go to the **Find Lawyer** menu
2. Filter by **Expertise**, **Minimum Rating**, and **Province/Area**
3. Tap **View Profile** to see background, education, license, experience, and client reviews

Lawyers with the **Featured Lawyer** badge are on a Lawslane membership plan. The badge does not certify their skill. Always check the profile and reviews when deciding.

## Verify a lawyer

Before hiring, check that the lawyer still holds a valid license.

1. Go to the **Verify Lawyer** menu
2. Enter the **License number** (เลขใบอนุญาต) or **Full Name**, then tap **Verify**
3. Check the status, such as **Active License** or **Suspended** (ถูกพัก)

If the name isn't found, tap **Check with the Lawyers Council** to check directly against the Lawyers Council's records.

> If you also need an interpreter, tap **Need an interpreter too?** on the lawyer's profile page. See [Legal interpreter service](/interpreter)
