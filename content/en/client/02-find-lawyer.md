## Find a lawyer

1. Go to the **Find Lawyer** menu
2. Filter by **Expertise**, **Minimum Rating**, and **Province/Area**
3. Tap **View Profile** to see background, education, license, experience, and client reviews

Lawyers with the **Featured Lawyer** badge are on a Lawslane membership plan. The badge does not certify their skill. Always check the profile and reviews when deciding.

## Book a consultation

Lawyers who have a personal web page (lawslane.com/p/…) show a **Book a consultation** (นัดปรึกษา) button on that page. It takes you to the **Book a consultation** (นัดหมายเพื่อปรึกษา) page.

1. **Pick a convenient date** (เลือกวันที่สะดวก) — days the lawyer doesn't take appointments can't be selected
2. **Briefly describe your problem** (อธิบายปัญหาของคุณโดยย่อ) so the lawyer can prepare
3. Tap **Send appointment request** (ส่งคำขอนัดหมาย (ไม่มีค่าใช้จ่ายผ่านระบบ))

Sending a request is free. Wait for the lawyer to accept it. Your appointment appears under **Dashboard** → **Upcoming Appointments** (นัดหมายที่กำลังจะมาถึง). If there is a fee, the lawyer will tell you in the chat and you transfer it to the lawyer directly.

## Verify a lawyer

Before hiring, check that the lawyer still holds a valid license.

1. Go to the **Verify Lawyer** menu
2. Enter the **License number** (เลขใบอนุญาต) or **Full Name**, then tap **Verify**
3. Check the status, such as **Active License** (ใบอนุญาตยังใช้งานได้), **License Suspended** (ถูกพักใบอนุญาต), **Struck Off** (ถูกถอดชื่อ), or **Named in a Lawyers Council license announcement** (พบชื่อในประกาศรับใบอนุญาตทนายความ). Results from an announcement show its date and a link to the original announcement

If the name isn't found, tap **Check with the Lawyers Council** to check directly against the Lawyers Council's records.

> If you also need an interpreter, tap **Need an interpreter too?** on the lawyer's profile page. See [Legal interpreter service](/interpreter)
