The Lawslane interpreter service helps with work that requires communicating across languages, such as meeting a lawyer, appearing in court, dealing with government agencies, or translating documents. The team finds a suitable interpreter for the job, so you don't have to choose one yourself.

## 3 steps

1. **Tell us about your job** (บอกงานของคุณ) — fill in the interpreter request form
2. **Talk to the team** (คุยกับทีมงาน) — the team asks for details and quotes a price in chat
3. **Get the right interpreter** (ได้ล่ามที่เหมาะกับงาน) — pay through the link, and the team confirms your interpreter

## Fill in the request form

Go to the **Legal interpreters** menu and scroll down to **Message the team to request an interpreter** (ทักทีมงานเพื่อขอล่าม) (login required).

- **Service**
- **From** and **To** (languages)
- **Date (if known)** (วันที่ (ถ้าทราบ))
- **Format** — on site or online
- **Location / province** (สถานที่ / จังหวัด)
- **Phone (optional)** (เบอร์โทรติดต่อ (ไม่บังคับ))
- **More details** (รายละเอียดเพิ่มเติม) — for example, what the job is about, how long it will take, or whether there are documents to translate

Tap **Send request and start chatting with the team** (ส่งคำขอและเริ่มแชทกับทีมงาน). A chat room with the team opens right away. You can see past requests on the dashboard.

> If you come from a lawyer's profile page (the **Need an interpreter too?** button), the team will see that you need an interpreter for talking to that lawyer
