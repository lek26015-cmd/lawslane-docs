## Legal documents

**Legal documents** (เอกสารกฎหมาย), opened from the **Quick services** (บริการด่วน) panel on the dashboard, is a library of downloadable templates, grouped into business contracts, employment contracts, real estate, family and inheritance, and powers of attorney. You can search by name. A template may have files in several languages (TH / EN / ZH) to choose from, and shows how many times it has been downloaded. You must log in before downloading.

## AI advisor

**AI advisor** (AI ที่ปรึกษา), also opened from the dashboard's **Quick services** (บริการด่วน) panel, lets you ask basic legal questions in a chat (login required).

> AI answers are general information, not legal advice. For decisions that really matter, [consult a lawyer on lawslane.com](/client/find-lawyer).
