## Legal documents

The **Legal documents** (เอกสารกฎหมาย) menu is a library of downloadable templates, grouped into business contracts, employment contracts, real estate, family and inheritance, and powers of attorney. You can search by name. You must log in before downloading.

## AI advisor

The **AI advisor** (AI ที่ปรึกษา) menu lets you ask basic legal questions in a chat (login required).

> AI answers are general information, not legal advice. For decisions that really matter, [consult a lawyer on lawslane.com](/client/find-lawyer).
