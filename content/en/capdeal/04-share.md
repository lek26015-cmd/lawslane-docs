---
title: Share a contract with the other party
description: Create a PIN-protected link so the other party can view and sign without an account
---

## Create a link

1. On the contract page, click **Share this contract** (แชร์สัญญานี้)
2. The **Set a PIN for security** (ตั้งรหัสผ่าน PIN เพื่อความปลอดภัย) switch is on by default. Enter a 4-digit PIN
3. Click **Create link and copy** (สร้างลิงก์และคัดลอก). The link is copied right away
4. Send the link to the other party by chat, **and tell them the PIN through a separate channel**, such as a phone call

> **Turning off the PIN is not recommended**, because anyone who gets the link can open it and sign in place of the other party.

If you create a new link, the old link stops working immediately. This is useful if you sent it to the wrong person.

## For the party receiving the link

The other party does not need to sign up.

1. Open the link, enter the **4-digit PIN** (PIN 4 หลัก), and click **View contract** (เข้าดูสัญญา)
2. Read the contract, download the PDF, and open attachments
3. Click **Sign (…)** (เซ็นชื่อ (…)) in your own box. The part in brackets is your role, which depends on the contract type, e.g. เซ็นชื่อ (ผู้รับจ้าง) for a contractor or เซ็นชื่อ (ผู้ซื้อ) for a buyer. Then follow the [signing steps](/capdeal/sign)

If the PIN is entered wrong 5 times, the link is locked for 15 minutes.
