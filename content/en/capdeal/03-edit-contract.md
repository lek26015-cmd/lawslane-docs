---
title: Edit contracts and attachments
description: Change the contract type, edit terms, create a revised version, and download a PDF
---

Open a contract from the dashboard (**View contract** (ดูสัญญา)). Only the contract owner can open this page. The other party must open it through the [share link](/capdeal/share).

## Edit terms (before anyone signs)

Click **Edit terms** (แก้ไขข้อตกลง). You can edit:

- **Contract type** (ประเภทสัญญา) — employment, service, sale, loan, NDA, or other. The party labels change with the type, e.g. buyer/seller
- **Parties** (คู่สัญญา), scope of work, price, deposit, deadline, and payment terms

When done, click **Save changes** (บันทึกการแก้ไข).

## Create a revised version (after someone has signed)

Once either party has signed, the terms are locked. To change the terms, click **Create revised version** (สร้างฉบับแก้ไข). The system copies the terms and attachments into a new, unsigned contract whose title ends with "(Revised)" (ฉบับแก้ไข). The original contract stays unchanged, and both parties must sign the new version again. A revised version also counts as a new contract in your quota.

## Download PDF

Click the **PDF** button on the contract page, or click **View full contract** (ดูสัญญาเต็มแผ่น) and then **Download as PDF** (ดาวน์โหลดเป็น PDF). On the Free plan, the PDF has a Lawslane watermark.

## Contract attachments

On a paid plan, you can click **Add attachment** (เพิ่มเอกสารแนบ) to attach supporting files, such as a quotation or design drawings. On the Free plan, you will see a **View plans** (ดูแพ็กเกจ) button instead.
