Cap & Deal ([capdeal.lawslane.com](https://capdeal.lawslane.com)) is built for freelancers, online shops, and anyone who agrees on work or a sale over chat. Just **cap** (screenshot) the chat, and AI reads it and drafts a contract for you. Then send a link so the other party can sign online, verified by OTP on their phone.

## How it works

1. **Cap & Deal** — Upload chat screenshots and let AI draft the contract ([how to](/capdeal/create-contract))
2. **Review and edit** — Fix party names, scope of work, price, and deadline ([how to](/capdeal/edit-contract))
3. **Share** — Create a PIN-protected link and send it to the other party ([how to](/capdeal/share))
4. **Sign** — Both parties draw their signatures and confirm with OTP ([how to](/capdeal/sign))

## Log in

Use the same account as lawslane.com. If you are already logged in on one site, you are logged in automatically on the other. The Cap & Deal login page supports Google, LINE, and email ([details](/start/signup-login)).

> Lawyer accounts are redirected to the lawyer dashboard on lawslane.com, because Cap & Deal is made for general customers.

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** shows your 5 most recent contracts with their status (**Draft** (ร่าง) / **Awaiting signature** (อยากเซ็น) / **Signed** (เซ็นแล้ว)). Click **View contract** (ดูสัญญา) to open one, or **Create new contract** (สร้างสัญญาใหม่) to start a new one. If you have no contracts yet, you will see the message "No contracts created with Cap & Deal yet" (ยังไม่มีสัญญาที่สร้างจากแคปดีล) and a **Start Cap & Deal now!** (เริ่มแคปแล้วดีลเลย!) button instead. The sidebar's **Quick services** (บริการด่วน) panel has shortcuts to **Cap & Deal**, **Legal documents** (เอกสารกฎหมาย), **AI advisor** (AI ที่ปรึกษา), and **Profile** (ข้อมูลส่วนตัว).
