---
title: Register as a lawyer on Lawslane
description: Complete a 4-step application, submit identity documents, and wait for the team to review
---

## Before you start, prepare

- Your lawyer license number
- A photo of your ID card and a photo of your lawyer license
- A profile photo that clearly shows your face
- A bank account for receiving fees. **The account name must match the applicant's name**

## Application steps

Go to the **For Lawyers** menu on lawslane.com and fill in the application. Use the **Next** / **Back** buttons to move between steps.

1. **Basic information** (ข้อมูลพื้นฐาน) — full name, email, password, and contact number
2. **Professional information** (ข้อมูลวิชาชีพ) — license number, service provinces, and expertise
3. **Payout information** (ข้อมูลการรับเงิน) — bank, account number, and LINE ID
4. **Identity verification** (ยืนยันตัวตน) — upload your ID card, license, and profile photo

Tap **Confirm and submit application** (ยืนยันการส่งใบสมัคร) to submit.

## After you submit

While the team reviews your documents, the dashboard shows the status **Documents under review** (อยู่ระหว่างการตรวจสอบเอกสาร). Once approved, your profile appears in the lawyer search and clients can message you.

> Identity documents are stored privately and separately. Only the team reviewing applications can view them, and they are never shown on public pages

Next time, log in on the **Lawyer** tab of the login page, using the email and password you set during sign-up.

## Register your name only, for client verification

If you do not want to take cases through Lawslane yet but want clients to find your name or license number on the **Verify lawyer** page, you can register your name for free. No account or documents needed.

1. Go to the **Verify lawyer** page and tap **Are you a lawyer? Register your name for clients to verify**
2. Enter your title and full name exactly as on your license, your license number in the format number/Buddhist year (e.g. 12345/2560), and your province of practice (optional)
3. Enter your phone number (email is optional) so the team can contact you if your details do not match the registry
4. Tick the consent box and tap **Submit registration**

The team checks your name and license number against the Lawyers Council of Thailand first. Once approved, clients can find your name on the Verify lawyer page.

> The page shows only your title, full name, license number and province. Your phone and email are never shown on the website and are deleted after approval
