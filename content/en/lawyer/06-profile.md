Your profile is the first thing clients see in lawyer search. It shows:

- **About** — a short introduction to the types of cases you handle and how you work
- **Education & License**
- **Experience** — work or cases you've handled (do not include anything that could identify a client)
- **Expertise** and **Service provinces** (จังหวัดที่ให้บริการ) — used in search filters, so choose what you actually take on
- **Reviews** — from clients after a case is closed

## Edit your profile

Go to **Manage Account** (จัดการบัญชี) from the profile picture menu, tap **Edit** on the **Profile Information** card, then tap **Save Changes** when you're done.

- Full name and profile picture
- The **Lawyer Information** section (ข้อมูลทนายความ) — phone number, office address, introduction / bio, service provinces, and specialty
- **Bank Account Information (For payments)** — can also be set on the **Payout account** (บัญชีรับเงิน) page in the dashboard. See [Receive payments from clients](/lawyer/payments)

You can't edit **Education** or **Experience** yourself. To change them, [contact the team](/help/contact).

> You can edit your **License Number** yourself only while your account is pending review or was rejected. Once approved, the field is locked. It must match the real license you submitted when registering. If you need to change it, [contact the team](/help/contact) so they can review your documents again

## Personal web page

The **Personal web page** (หน้าเว็บส่วนตัว, Pro) menu in the dashboard builds an introduction page at lawslane.com/p/your-name to share on LINE, Facebook, or your business card.

- **Look** tab (หน้าตา) — set the **Link name** (ชื่อลิงก์; a-z, 0-9 and hyphens), display name, and short tagline. Choose one of 4 **Templates** (Classic / Modern / Minimal / Elegant), a **Heading font**, and a **Theme colour**. You can add a cover image, logo, and profile photo (optional)
- **Sections** tab (ส่วนของหน้า) — turn each section on or off, reorder them with the arrows, and rename headings. You write sections such as About, Services, Team, and FAQ yourself; Experience & education, Expertise, Reviews, and Office hours are pulled from your account and appointment schedule
- **Contact** tab (ติดต่อ) — fill in only the channels you want to publish, such as phone, email, or LINE ID. The **Chat with lawyer** (แชทกับทนาย) and **Book a consultation** (นัดปรึกษา) buttons through Lawslane are always shown

Your name, license number, verification status, and review rating are pulled from your account automatically and can't be edited on this page.

Tap **Save draft** (บันทึกแบบร่าง) or **Publish** (เผยแพร่).

- You can publish only after your lawyer account has been approved
- On the Free plan you can design the page and save drafts, but publishing requires the Pro or Firm plan. If your plan expires, the link sends visitors to your regular lawyer profile instead
- If your text promises results or makes exaggerated claims, you'll be asked to fix it before saving

## Appointment schedule

Go to **Appointment schedule** (ตารางนัดหมาย) to set working hours, working days, and special holidays.
