## Subscribe to a plan

1. Go to **Plan** and choose a plan
2. Tick to accept the terms. The plan **renews automatically** every billing cycle
3. Tap **Subscribe to this plan** (สมัครแพลนนี้) and pay by card through Stripe

## Change card or cancel

Tap **Manage payment** (จัดการการชำระเงิน) on the Plan page. This opens a Stripe page where you can change your card, view receipts, or cancel renewal. If you cancel, you keep your current plan benefits until the end of the billing cycle you've already paid for.
