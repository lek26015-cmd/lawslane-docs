Your profile picture in the top-right menu, on the Manage Account page, and in the dashboard gets a coloured ring and plan badge: Pro has a gold ring with a **Pro** badge, and Firm has a dark-gold-to-orange ring with a black **บริษัท** (Firm) badge. The Free plan has no ring or badge.

See the latest prices on the **Plan** page in the dashboard.

> If your plan expires or is cancelled, the cases and invoices you created are hidden (not deleted) and come back when you renew. You can still close cases that clients hired you for through chat.

Your current plan is shown by the plan badge at the top of the dashboard menu. If the Lawslane team granted you a plan (for example, a trial), the badge says **ได้รับจากทีม Lawslane** (granted by the Lawslane team) and the Plan page shows when it expires.

## Subscribe to a plan

1. Go to **Plan** and choose a plan
2. Tick to accept the terms. The plan **renews automatically** every billing cycle
3. Tap **Subscribe to this plan** (สมัครแพลนนี้) and pay by card through Stripe

## Change card or cancel

Tap **Manage payment** (จัดการการชำระเงิน) on the Plan page. This opens a Stripe page where you can change your card, view receipts, or cancel renewal. If you cancel, you keep your current plan benefits until the end of the billing cycle you've already paid for.
