---
title: Receive payments from clients
description: Set up a payout account, confirm when payment arrives, and issue invoices
---

> Clients transfer fees directly to your account. Lawslane does not receive or hold this money and takes no fee, so there is no balance or withdrawal in the system

## Set up a payout account

1. Go to **Payout account** in the dashboard (the **Payout account and tax invoices** (บัญชีรับเงินและใบกำกับภาษี) page)
2. Enter the **Bank**, **Account Number**, and **Account Name**. **The account name must match the name you registered as a lawyer**
3. Tap **Save account** (บันทึกบัญชี)

Clients see this account when they tap to transfer fees in the chat room. If you haven't set it up, clients see a message telling them to ask you how to pay instead.

If you issue tax invoices as a juristic person, fill in the **Juristic person details** (ข้อมูลนิติบุคคล) card on the same page.

## Confirm when payment arrives

When a client reports a transfer, you get an in-app notification and an email (**Client reports they have transferred money to you** (ลูกความแจ้งว่าโอนเงินให้คุณแล้ว)).

1. Open that case's chat room and look at the **Awaiting payment confirmation** (รอยืนยันรับเงิน) section in the sidebar
2. Tap **View proof** (ดูหลักฐาน) if the client attached one, then **check the amount in your own banking app**
3. Tap **Confirm payment received** (ยืนยันได้รับเงิน), then confirm again

> Don't confirm based on a slip image alone. Always check the amount in your real account first. If a client transferred but didn't report it, you can confirm it yourself

For installments, confirm each one separately. Confirmed amounts count toward **This month's income** (รายได้เดือนนี้) on the Overview page.

## Invoices

Go to **Invoices** (ใบแจ้งหนี้) to issue invoices to clients and see the **Amount paid by client** (ยอดที่ลูกความชำระแล้ว).
