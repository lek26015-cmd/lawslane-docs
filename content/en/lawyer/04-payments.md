---
title: Receive payments from clients
description: Fees are paid through Lawslane. Track your earnings, set up a payout account, and issue invoices
---

> From 19 Oct 2026, clients pay every lawyer fee (case fee, installments, and any additional fee at case close) **into Lawslane's account**. Lawslane holds the money until you close the case, deducts a platform fee (GP), currently 10% of the fee, and transfers the net amount to your payout account. Clients pay only the amount shown; the GP comes out of the lawyer's share

## How the money flows

1. The client transfers to the Lawslane account and attaches the slip. Once paid, you get a notification (ลูกความชำระเงินเข้าระบบแล้ว, "The client has paid into the system")
2. You finish the work and [close the case](/lawyer/cases)
3. After the case is closed, the net amount goes into Lawslane's payout queue
4. When Lawslane transfers it, you get the notification **Lawslane has paid your fees** (Lawslane โอนค่าบริการให้คุณแล้ว) with the transfer reference

You no longer need to confirm receipt yourself.

> If you cancel a case before closing it, you won't receive that case's money, because Lawslane refunds it to the client

## Track payments in the chat room

In the case panel of the chat room, each installment has a status badge:

- **Paid into the system** (ชำระเข้าระบบแล้ว) — the client has paid into Lawslane's account
- **Partly paid** (ชำระบางส่วน) — the client has paid part of this installment
- **Awaiting team slip review** (รอทีมงานตรวจสลิป) — the client attached a slip; the Lawslane team is checking it

The client may pay an installment, the case fee, or an additional fee in several parts (at least ฿1,000 each). An item that isn't fully paid shows **Paid ฿… · under review ฿… · remaining ฿…** (ชำระแล้ว ฿… · รอตรวจ ฿… · คงเหลือ ฿…) and becomes paid once the client has paid the full amount.

The **Unpaid fees** (ค่าบริการที่ยังไม่ได้ชำระ) section lists items marked "Waiting for the client to pay into Lawslane's account" (รอลูกความชำระเข้าบัญชี Lawslane).

The **Confirm payment received** (ยืนยันได้รับเงิน) button only appears on items with the **Client reported a direct transfer** (ลูกความแจ้งโอนตรง) badge, meaning items the client reported as transferred directly to you before the change. Check the amount in your own account before confirming.

## Earnings and payout account page

Go to **Payout account** in the dashboard (the **Earnings and payout account** (รายได้และบัญชีรับเงิน) page).

| Summary card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting case close** (รอปิดเคส) | The client has paid; waiting for you to close the case |
| **Awaiting Lawslane transfer** (รอ Lawslane โอน) | The case is closed; waiting for Lawslane to pay you |
| **Paid to you** (โอนให้คุณแล้ว) | Lawslane has transferred it to your account |
| **GP deducted** (GP ที่หักแล้ว) | Platform fees deducted |

The **Fees paid through Lawslane** (ค่าบริการที่ชำระผ่าน Lawslane) list shows each item's net amount, full amount, GP percentage, and status: awaiting team slip review / awaiting case close / awaiting Lawslane transfer to you / paid to you / case cancelled · awaiting client refund / refunded to client / slip rejected (รอทีมงานตรวจสลิป / รอปิดเคส / รอ Lawslane โอนให้คุณ / โอนให้คุณแล้ว / เคสยกเลิก · รอคืนลูกความ / คืนลูกความแล้ว / สลิปไม่ผ่าน). If the client pays in parts, each part is a separate row tagged **(paid in parts, of ฿…)** (แบ่งจ่าย จากยอด ฿…) showing the installment's full amount, and GP is deducted per row.

## Set up a payout account

In the **Payout account** (บัญชีรับเงิน) card ("Lawslane pays your fees into this account"):

1. Enter the **Bank**, **Account Number**, and **Account Name**. **The account name must match the name you registered as a lawyer**
2. Tap **Save account** (บันทึกบัญชี)

Without an account, Lawslane can't pay your fees out to you.

If you issue tax invoices as a juristic person, fill in the **Juristic person details** (ข้อมูลนิติบุคคล) card on the same page.

## Invoices

Go to **Invoices** (ใบแจ้งหนี้) to issue invoices to clients and see the **Amount paid by client** (ยอดที่ลูกความชำระแล้ว).
