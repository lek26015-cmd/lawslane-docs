---

## Quote to open a case

1. In the chat room with the client, tap **Quote to open a case** (เสนอราคาเปิดคดี)
2. Enter a title, scope of work, and total fee. To split payment, set it up as installments
3. Tap **Create engagement contract** (สร้างสัญญาการจ้างงาน) and review the draft contract
4. Send it to the client. The client sees a **Case offer** (ข้อเสนอเริ่มดำเนินคดี) card with the contract to sign

For installments, tap **Send payment request** (ส่งคำขอชำระเงิน) when each installment is due. The client transfers directly to your account. See [Receive payments from clients](/lawyer/payments)

## Legal Vault

- **Upload case documents** (อัปโหลดเอกสารคดี) — supports images and PDFs, up to 15MB per file
- **Client document access link** (ลิงก์เข้าถึงเอกสารสำหรับลูกความ) — create a link that lets the client view selected documents

The chat room also has a **Document Vault** tab where you and the client can upload files together.

## Close a case

When the work is done, tap **Close with summary** (ปิดสรุปเคส), write a summary of the work, then tap **Send summary and close case** (ส่งสรุปและปิดเคส). The client receives an invitation to rate and review.

## Cancel a case

If you need to cancel a case where the client has already transferred fees, **refunds are your direct responsibility**, because the money went into your account, not through Lawslane.
