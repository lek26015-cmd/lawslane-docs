Lawslane has several sites that do different things, but they share one user account. Sign up once and you can use them all.

| Site | Best for | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [lawslane.com](https://lawslane.com) | Clients who want to consult a lawyer, and lawyers taking on work | [Clients](/client) · [Lawyers](/lawyer) |
| [lawslane.com/interpreters](https://lawslane.com/th/interpreters) | People who need an interpreter for legal matters | [Interpreter service](/interpreter) |
| [capdeal.lawslane.com](https://capdeal.lawslane.com) | Freelancers and shops who want to make contracts from chat | [Cap & Deal](/capdeal) |
| [wittaya.lawslane.com](https://wittaya.lawslane.com) | Law students and exam candidates | [Lawslane Wittaya](/wittaya) |

## Signing in across sites

- **lawslane.com and Cap & Deal** — sign in on one site and the other signs you in automatically (for up to 5 days)
- **Lawslane Wittaya** — you need to sign in again, but you can use the same email, password, or Google account. No new sign-up needed

> **Lawyer** accounts are mainly used on lawslane.com. If you open Cap & Deal with a lawyer account, you'll be sent back to the lawyer dashboard
