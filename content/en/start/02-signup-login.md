## Sign up

1. Tap **Sign up** (สมัครสมาชิก) at the top right of the site
2. Choose **Sign up with Google** (สมัครสมาชิกด้วย Google), or enter your full name, email, and password
3. Tick to accept the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**
4. Pass the bot check, then tap **Sign up** (สมัครสมาชิก)

> If you are a **Lawyer**, sign up through the **For Lawyers** page instead, because you need to submit identity documents. See [Register as a lawyer on Lawslane](/lawyer/register)

## Log in

| Method | lawslane.com | Cap & Deal | Wittaya |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| LINE | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Email + password | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

If you log in with Google or LINE for the first time, an account is created for you automatically, and you are deemed to have accepted the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Lawyers log in on the **Lawyer** tab (ทนายความ) of the lawslane.com login page. The tab takes you to the lawyer login page, which accepts only the email and password from sign-up (there is no Google or LINE button).

## Forgot your password

1. On the login page, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email, then tap **Send reset link**
3. Open the email, tap the link, and set a new password

If you don't see the email, check your spam or Promotions folder.
