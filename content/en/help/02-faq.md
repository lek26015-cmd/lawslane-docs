## Account

**Do I need to sign up separately for each site?**
No. One account works on every site in the Lawslane family. See [One account for every site](/start/one-account).

**What if I forget my password?**
Click **Forgot your password?** on the login page. See [Sign up and log in](/start/signup-login).

## Clients

**Does consulting a lawyer cost anything?**
Sending an initial consultation message is free. If you want the lawyer to continue the work, the lawyer will quote a price in the chat first.

**How do I pay the lawyer?**
Transfer directly to the lawyer's account. Lawslane does not hold your money. See [Paying for lawyer services](/client/payment).

**Can the AI LAlin (ลลิน) replace a lawyer?**
No. LAlin (ลลิน) helps you understand your situation at a basic level, but it is not legal advice.

**How do I know a lawyer is really licensed?**
Use the **Verify Lawyer** page. See [Find and verify lawyers](/client/find-lawyer).

## Lawyers

**Does Lawslane take a fee from my service charges?**
No. Clients transfer directly to your account. Lawslane's revenue comes from optional membership plans.

**How do I withdraw money?**
You don't need to, because the money goes into your account from the start. See [Receiving payment from clients](/lawyer/payments).

## Cap & Deal

**Does the other party need to sign up?**
No. They open the share link, enter the PIN, and sign with OTP.

**Are contracts signed in Cap & Deal actually valid?**
Cap & Deal records signatures together with OTP verification, IP address, device, and a content fingerprint to use as evidence. However, the legal effect depends on the contract content and the facts of each case. For important work, have a lawyer review the contract first.
