## Avoid scams

- **Always check the account name before transferring.** Lawyer fees must be transferred to an account in that lawyer's name. Interpreter fees are transferred only to the Lawslane account shown in the payment link
- Check a lawyer's license status on the [Verify Lawyer](/client/find-lawyer) page
- The Lawslane team **never asks for your password, OTP code, or card details** by chat, phone, or email
- In Cap & Deal, send the PIN through a different channel from the link, and never share your OTP code with anyone

## Your data

- Lawyers' identity documents, such as ID cards and licenses, are stored privately and never shown on public pages
- Files in a case's document library can only be opened by people in the case, or by people who received a share link from the lawyer
- Read the details in the [Privacy Policy](https://lawslane.com/th/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://lawslane.com/th/terms)

If you think someone has accessed your account without permission, change your password immediately, then [notify the team](/help/contact).
