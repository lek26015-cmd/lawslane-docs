## Report an issue

1. Log in, then go to the [Help Center](https://lawslane.com/th/help)
2. In the **Report an Issue** form, enter the **Case Number** if you have one, then choose an issue type, such as a communication problem with a lawyer, a payment problem, a technical problem, or dissatisfaction with service quality
3. Write the details, then click **Submit Issue**

The team is notified immediately. You can see issues you reported under **Dashboard** → **Reported Issue Tickets**. Open one to see its status, attach more files, and continue talking with the team.

If the problem is in a case you are working on, click **Report an Issue** in that case's chat room, so the team can see the case context too.

## Request a Wittaya plan upgrade or other matters

Send it through the same form. Choose the **Other** type and say what you need.
